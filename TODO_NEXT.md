## 🛑 Don't bother (skip)

These were intentionally not built. Don't reintroduce without strong reason:

- ❌ Specific dollar pricing on page (App Store is authoritative; prices change)
- ❌ Fake testimonials (FTC risk, can't be undone if caught)
- ❌ Live chat widget (overkill for iOS app)
- ❌ Email newsletter capture (App Store is your channel)
- ❌ Female imagery anywhere (target audience constraint)
- ❌ 3-tier pricing card with "Most popular" badge (contradicts the no-pricing decision)

## ⚙️ Maintenance & sync

### When Convex pose photos change
1. `cd /tmp` → fresh dir
2. `npm init -y && npm i convex`
3. `npx convex export --deployment glad-spaniel-840 --include-file-storage --path snapshot.zip`
4. Unzip, copy categorized images to `public/poses-v2/<category>/`
5. Regenerate `lib/poses.ts` manifest

### When updating legal pages
- Bump "Last updated" date at top of `/privacy` and `/terms`
- If material changes — also update bottom "effective as of" line

### When adding a new section
- Add to `app/page.tsx` in correct position
- Use the `pill-gradient pill-gradient-upper` pattern for the kicker (not the old `.kicker` class)
- Use `font-serif` for headlines with `<em className="gradient-text">punchword</em>` italic accent
- Mobile: `max-w-md` container · Desktop: `lg:max-w-5xl` or `lg:max-w-6xl`
- Add to sitemap.ts if it's a new route, not a section
